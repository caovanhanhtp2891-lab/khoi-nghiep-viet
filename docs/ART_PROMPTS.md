Công cụ: built-in image generation. Hai ảnh được dùng làm asset của dự án; ảnh người dùng là reference phong cách cho nền phố, không phải edit target.

## Vietnam street

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait 2D game street background. Create a NEW charming hand-drawn Vietnamese neighborhood street in flat warm cartoon mobile tycoon game style with dark brown outlines, watercolor/pencil texture, cream, terracotta, mustard, jade palette. Portrait 1024x1536. Eye-level slightly elevated side view, not bird's-eye map. Three narrow Vietnamese tube houses with balconies, tiled awnings, shutters, plants, rooftop water tanks, tangled overhead power cables. Ground-floor grocery store on left, breakfast food stall/store center, cafe right. Tiny Vietnamese flags, plastic stools, potted bougainvillea, parked bicycle at left. Buildings occupy upper 55%; broad empty beige sidewalk for animated people and stalls; gray asphalt road below. No people, interface, speech bubbles, text/lettering, watermark. Blank store signs for code to label. Do not reproduce reference characters or UI.

## Character atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for a 2D Vietnamese street tycoon game. Exact uniform grid of four columns and two rows; eight equal cells, no borders/labels. Row 1: same friendly young Vietnamese man age 18, short dark hair, expressive eyes/eyebrows/nose/smiling mouth, jade green jacket, cream shirt, dark trousers, brown sneakers. Row 2: same friendly young Vietnamese woman age 18, dark shoulder-length hair, expressive face, terracotta jacket, cream shirt, navy trousers and sneakers. Four walking frames each: neutral, left foot forward, passing, right foot forward. All three-quarter front facing slightly right, visible faces, same scale, feet aligned, transparent margins. Flat hand-drawn cartoon, rounded proportions, warm brown outlines, pastel coloring, pencil texture. No props/shadows/text/backdrop/UI/grid/watermark. All sprites separated by transparent space.

Ảnh atlas thực tế có kích thước 1254×1254; code tính frame theo kích thước thực thay vì giả định kích thước prompt. Đây là mô tả prompt sản xuất được lưu để tái sử dụng, không đảm bảo lần tạo tiếp theo ra pixel giống hệt.
